IGCZ

A compact, byte-exact encoding of IGC files. About 20× smaller than IGC

    Files never leave your computer: conversion runs in your browser (WebAssembly build of the Zig library).

    How it works

    • Each column (time,lat,lng,altitude) predicts its next value. Positions and altitudes guess "same change as last time", extensions guess "same value as last time".
    • An adaptive arithmetic coder stores the errors. Smooth flight makes small, regular errors, which often cost well under one bit.
    • Everything else stays text. Headers, events, signature and malformed lines are kept verbatim and deflated with a preset dictionary of common header text.
